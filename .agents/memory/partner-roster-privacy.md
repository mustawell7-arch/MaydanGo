---
name: Partner roster privacy
description: Scope member identity and membership details shown to partner staff.
---

Members may optionally provide a profile photo with explicit, versioned consent. Only partner staff authorized for the specific booking may view it during that booking's active check-in window, and only while the member's required service consent is current. Staff compare the photo visually; never use automated face recognition or biometric matching. A photo must not be required to book or check in. Keep payment status out until the final payment milestone.

**Why:** The user chose staff visual comparison at check-in, not automated face matching, and wants members who do not provide a photo to remain eligible.

**How to apply:** Scope photo delivery to the authorized partner and booking, and stop showing it outside the check-in window. Do not use a Clerk/social avatar as a substitute for the member's opted-in photo. Preserve the neutral fallback and defer payment status to the final payment milestone.
