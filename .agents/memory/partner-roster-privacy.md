---
name: Partner roster privacy
description: Scope member identity and membership details shown to partner staff.
---

The partner attendance roster may show a member’s account name, available profile image, and membership plan or tier only to staff authorized for that partner’s roster. Use existing Clerk profile data; do not collect a new photograph for this view. Keep payment status out until the final payment milestone.

**Why:** The PDF asks partners to identify booked members, while the user requires privacy and deferred payment work.

**How to apply:** Keep identity lookup behind the existing partner attendance authorization and booking scope. Use a neutral fallback when profile details are missing, and do not add payment fields before the final payment milestone.
