---
name: MaydanGo corporate portal scope
description: User-approved boundaries for company HR and employee portal access.
---

For the initial corporate portal, HR manages only its own employee roster and invitations. Employees see only their own company enrollment and membership summary. HR must not see individual booking, attendance, or other member activity. Roster removal does not grant or revoke a MaydanGo membership; an active company membership must be handled separately by MaydanGo. Keep invoices, payments, budgets, contributions, and usage reporting out of this initial scope.

**Why:** The user selected employee management only and deferred payment work until the final stage.

**How to apply:** Keep corporate roles and routes company-scoped. Add finance or analytics only when explicitly requested as a later milestone.
