---
name: Fitness challenges
description: Member-private challenge progress rules and its trusted activity sources.
---

Fitness challenges are opt-in and member-private. A member can self-report workout days, and partner-confirmed, settlement-eligible attendance after joining can count automatically. Count each Iraq-local calendar day once even when both sources occur on the same day. Challenge progress does not affect payments, membership credits, or access, and it is not shown on public leaderboards or in the challenge-management view.

Leaving a challenge deletes that member's participation and self-reported challenge entries, but does not delete normal attendance records. Include the member's own challenge participation and progress dates in their privacy export.

**Why:** The user chose both self-reported activity and partner-confirmed attendance as progress sources. The implementation keeps those signals private and makes no payment or membership changes.

**How to apply:** Use Iraq-local dates for activity counting, retain the attendance trust rules, and explain what leaving removes before confirming the action. Do not add public rankings, admin-level individual progress, or reward effects without a new user decision.
