---
name: Course assignments
description: Practice-task authoring and member progress privacy for MaydanGo courses.
---

MaydanGo course assignments are self-directed practice tasks. Assigned instructors may create, edit, and remove localized task instructions, but member completion is private to each member and is not visible to instructors.

**Why:** The user chose practice tracking only rather than submissions for instructor review.

**How to apply:** Keep assignment completion scoped to the signed-in member and do not expose member task status, responses, or completion lists in instructor APIs or screens.
