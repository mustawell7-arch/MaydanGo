---
name: Partner activity approval
description: How per-partner activity publishing modes relate to partner onboarding review.
---

Partner onboarding approval remains required in every publishing mode. The platform-default mode follows the global activity-review setting; activity-approval-required forces review for every activity; trusted-direct-publish allows only an already-approved partner to publish its own activities without a second review.

**Why:** Section 29 needs partner-level publishing controls, but trusted status must not bypass partner vetting or safety restrictions.

**How to apply:** Keep changes to activity review separate from partner onboarding, role authorization, and facility safety checks.
