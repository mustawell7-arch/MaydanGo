description: Product audience, regional coverage, localization, and brand boundary for MaydanGo.

MaydanGo serves people across Iraq, including the Kurdistan Region, southern Iraq, western Iraq, and other regions. Arabic, Kurdish, and English are required product languages. Use culturally respectful, regionally inclusive imagery and motivating copy. Urban Sports Club is a reference for the multi-partner membership purpose only; MaydanGo must have its own brand and product expression.

**Why:** The user explicitly set the platform's target market and languages and asked that the experience consider all Iraqi communities.

**How to apply:** Keep city discovery, content, partner onboarding, messaging, and visual choices inclusive of the whole Iraqi market; do not default the product to Baghdad alone.

The user wants nobody to feel discriminated against or disrespected. Online training from home, including for women and others who stay at home, and bookable online coaching are core product needs, not secondary extras.

**Why:** The user explicitly emphasized respectful inclusion and at-home access alongside physical memberships.

**How to apply:** Give physical and online services clear access paths; describe session eligibility neutrally and avoid assuming a person's abilities, preferences, or access based on their region or gender.

The user wants heritage from across Iraq represented in MaydanGo, including the eight-point Islamic geometric motif, Assyrian winged bull, Ishtar Gate, Erbil Citadel, Ur's ziggurat, Hanging Gardens, Hatra, and western Iraqi heritage.

**Why:** The user requested recognizable heritage from Kurdistan, the south, the west, and other Iraqi regions so the app feels welcoming and representative.

**How to apply:** The user will source the photos. Do not create replacement illustrations; integrate the user's chosen photos when provided. Treat sacred or religious patterns as heritage rather than national or political emblems, and identify historical reconstructions clearly.
