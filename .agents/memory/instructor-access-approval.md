---
name: Instructor access approval
description: MaydanGo instructor portal access and visibility rules.
---

An instructor must sign in and request portal access. A MaydanGo administrator reviews the request, approves or declines it with a reason, and explicitly assigns courses and future sessions. Approval alone does not grant broad operations access. Instructors may view only assigned course summaries, assigned future session details, and their own team-support conversations; never expose member, attendance, reservation, recording, or session-join data.

**Why:** The user chose “request, then manager approval” and said instructors should see their own courses and upcoming courses and chat with the MaydanGo team.

**How to apply:** Keep instructor access restricted to dedicated routes and explicit assignments. Do not add the instructor role to partner, finance, or general administrator allowlists.
