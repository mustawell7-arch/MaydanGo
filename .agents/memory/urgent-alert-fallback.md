name: Urgent report fallback
description: The selected backup destination and current in-app-only behavior for urgent safety alerts.
---

The public enquiry mailbox was selected as the intended fallback destination for urgent safety alerts, but the Gmail sender connection was dismissed. The current fallback is an in-app staff queue status only; do not claim an email was sent or include report details in an external notice unless an approved sender is connected.

**Why:** The user chose the enquiry mailbox, then chose to continue without connecting Gmail. Safety reports must not be silently sent through an unapproved channel.

**How to apply:** Keep the ticket visible to authorized staff and explain that external email fallback is unavailable until an email sender is explicitly connected and verified.
