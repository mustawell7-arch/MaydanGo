---
name: Guardian verification
description: MaydanGo's handling of guardian requirements for family and children's sessions.
---

Partners verify guardian consent on-site for participants under 18 and send a photographed copy to MaydanGo. The secure transfer, access, and retention process must be defined before rollout.

**Why:** The user specifies an 18+ minimum age, with guardian consent required for under-18 participants and a copy sent to MaydanGo.

**How to apply:** Require the partner's on-site check. Do not collect copies through the general website enquiry form; establish a secure, access-limited transfer and retention path before accepting or storing copies.
