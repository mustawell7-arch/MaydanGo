---
name: Guardian verification
description: MaydanGo's handling of guardian requirements for family and children's sessions.
---

Partners verify guardian requirements on-site. MaydanGo does not collect guardian identity or consent information.

**Why:** The user chose venue-based verification instead of an in-app guardian approval flow.

**How to apply:** Make provider-defined guardian requirements clear to members before booking or attending, and keep guardian data out of MaydanGo accounts and APIs.
