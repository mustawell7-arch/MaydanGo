---
name: Loyalty points
description: Earning, privacy, and payment boundaries for the MaydanGo loyalty-points milestone.
---

Award one point for each partner-confirmed, settlement-eligible live visit and each course whose lessons are all completed. A member sees only their own points and history. Self-reported workouts do not earn points. Points are non-cash, non-redeemable, and must not change membership access, visit credits, discounts, or payments until reward rules and the payment phase are explicitly approved. Existing eligible visit and course-completion records are included in the member's calculated balance and data export.

**Why:** The user chose visits and course completions as earning activities, and payment remains deferred until the final milestone.

**How to apply:** Keep points derived from qualifying source records, show them only to the account holder, and exclude pending, disputed, void, ineligible, non-live visits and incomplete courses. Do not award for self-reported challenge activity or build redemption without a new decision.
