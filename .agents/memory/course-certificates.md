---
name: Course certificates
description: Certificate approval and learner visibility rules for MaydanGo online courses.
---

MaydanGo course certificates are issued only after an assigned instructor approves a learner who has completed every lesson. Instructors may see only the learner's display name and completion date for assigned courses; they must not see contact, booking, or attendance details. The certificate is a course-completion record, not an accredited qualification.

**Why:** The user selected instructor approval only and approved the smallest useful learner visibility for certificate review.

**How to apply:** Keep completion verification server-side, restrict candidate lists and issuance to explicit instructor-course assignments, and show the issued certificate only to its learner.
