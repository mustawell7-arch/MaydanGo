---
name: Sponsored conversion attribution
description: Privacy-conscious attribution and deduplication for sponsored placements.
---

Count a sponsored conversion only after the server confirms the related booking or course enrolment exists and was created during the campaign. Deduplicate by the booking or enrolment record ID; do not store a member identifier in campaign analytics.

**Why:** Campaign reports need trustworthy conversion totals without creating a separate user-level advertising history.

**How to apply:** Use this for any new sponsored placement type or conversion-reporting surface.
