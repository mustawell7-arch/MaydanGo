name: Partner operations platform
description: The user's chosen platform for MaydanGo partner and admin operations.
---

MaydanGo partner tools belong in a web portal.

**Why:** the user selected a partner web portal when choosing where the PDF's partner/admin tools should live.

**How to apply:** extend the existing MaydanGo web portal for partner operations rather than placing those tools in the native app.

Before a partner application can be approved or launched, an authorized staff member must record an approved initial hygiene and safety inspection. Inspection findings and corrective actions should be visible to the applicant; an unresolved inspection blocks approval.

**Why:** the user chose to require an inspection before partner approval.

**How to apply:** retain each inspection as history, require complete checklist results, and prevent approval or launch until the latest inspection is approved.

Facility quality review thresholds are three linked facility/safety support tickets in a rolling 30-day window, or a published verified-review average below 3.5 after at least 10 reviews. These are human-review prompts, not automatic enforcement.

**Why:** the user selected these complaint and rating thresholds for MaydanGo.

**How to apply:** count only facility/safety tickets linked to a member booking or check-in; use published reviews from confirmed visits, and do not automatically suspend facilities or change bookings based on either signal.

Mystery visits are recorded after completion as unannounced quality assessments. The partner can review findings after submission; there is no advance notice or scheduling/assignment workflow.

**Why:** the PDF names mystery visits without defining a scheduling process, so the initial workflow stays limited to auditable completed inspections.

**How to apply:** do not notify the partner before a mystery visit is recorded; ask before adding visit scheduling, external auditors, or a separate assignment workflow.
